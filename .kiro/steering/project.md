# Nómina360

Nómina360 is a production-oriented SaaS payroll platform for Colombian companies.

This is not a disposable prototype. Architectural decisions must support long-term commercial use.

## Product priorities

In this order:

1. Payroll and financial correctness
2. Colombian legal compliance
3. Data integrity
4. Security
5. Auditability
6. Maintainability
7. Automated testing
8. Scalability
9. Performance
10. User experience

## Product scope

The platform currently includes or is evolving toward:

- Company management
- Users and authentication
- Employees
- Payroll periods
- Payroll novelties
- Payroll calculation
- Payroll approval and closing
- Employment termination
- Reports
- Audit logging

Future commercial capabilities include:

- PILA
- IBC rules
- ARL
- compensation funds
- parafiscals
- withholding tax
- garnishments and payroll deductions
- salary history
- accumulated balances
- benefits history
- indemnities
- corrections and reliquidations
- production observability
- disaster recovery
